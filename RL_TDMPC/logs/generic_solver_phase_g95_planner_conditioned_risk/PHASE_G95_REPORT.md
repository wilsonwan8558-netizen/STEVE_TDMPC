# Phase G9.5 — planner-conditioned counterfactual H=2 risk

**Classification: B — planner-conditioned training helps some prospective plan decisions but remains insufficient for G10.** The frozen Generic solver, contact-force definition, TD-MPC2 step-280000 controller/planner, E1 current-state representation, H=2 direct target, quantile levels, and `F_lim = 3.685331040756903` nominal N were unchanged. No online filtering or controller change was made.

## Frozen protocol and provenance

- Runtime: `steve_tdmpc` at `/home/bizon/anaconda3/envs/steve_tdmpc/bin/python`, PyTorch `2.7.1+cu128`, CUDA `12.8`, `cuda:0` on NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090; no CPU fallback. Controller checkpoint SHA256: `476564f8d301ae0c76c418d8e61a4129037b30de7c6872377b563c70e84acd99`.
- Frozen simulator: GenericConstraintSolver with projected Gauss-Seidel, tolerance `1e-4`, 2,000 maximum iterations, constraint-force computation on, contact friction `mu=0.001`, and SOFA `dt=0.006 s`. Distal force is reconstructed as `J_contact^T lambda / 0.006 s` over approximately the first 3 mm of guidewire. Each 0.132 s RL interval contains 22 SOFA substeps; its force value is the maximum distal force over those substeps.
- The unchanged TD-MPC2 evaluation planner is a stochastic CEM with horizon 3, 512 length-3 action sequences per iteration (488 clipped Gaussian samples plus 24 policy-generated prefixes), six iterations, and 64 elites. Its Gaussian is initialized at zero on an episode's first step; otherwise the prior optimized mean is shifted, with a zero final action. Initial standard deviation is 2; elite exponential weights use temperature 0.5; updated standard deviations are clipped to `[0.05, 2]`. Actions are normalized 2D float32 vectors clipped componentwise to `[-1, 1]`. The executed action is the first action of the optimized mean. `set_seed(seed)`, action-space seeding, and ordered resets control the planner stream. A passive wrapper copied the sixth/final `_estimate_value` input and scores without adding RNG calls or changing its return value. See `planner_candidate_protocol.json` for the exact extraction contract.
- For each selected state, the predeclared label-free subset contains two highest-score final-population sequences, two near the median score rank, two lowest-score sequences, two greedy max-min diverse sequences in first-two-action 4D space, and the final optimized mean plan: **nine plans/state**. Score ties use original population index. Only each plan's original first two actions are passed to the H=2 risk study; candidate sampling and optimization are not changed. The second action is executed open-loop in a true Generic-SOFA fork, **without replanning**.
- E1 is the frozen G9 128–128 mapper from three 14D observations at decision indices `d−2:d` and the two preceding 2D actions to the estimated current 38D physical state (46D input, train-only normalization). The risk input is the frozen 256D current-observation TD-MPC2 latent, this 38D E1 estimate, and the explicit original 2×2 candidate action sequence: **298D**. No future observation, future behavior action, true current force, or privileged physical state is an evaluation-time input.
- The direct target remains `R2(d,a_d,a_{d+1}) = max(F_{d+1}, F_{d+2})`, where each `F` is the maximum reconstructed distal-contact force over all SOFA substeps of one RL control interval. A fork ending after one interval has no `F2` or `R2`; it is retained as censored evidence and excluded from complete-target training/metrics. True-safe means `R2 <= F_lim`. A predicted-safe plan has **validation-corrected** `Q95(R2) <= F_lim`; the correction is the frozen nonnegative 95th-percentile validation residual.
- Both model families use the exact G9 E1/H=2 64–64 ReLU noncrossing Q50/Q90/Q95/Q99 quantile net, equal-weight pinball loss, Adam at 0.001, batch size 512, maximum 60 epochs, patience 8, and validation-only checkpoint/calibration. Both use the **same frozen G9 behavior-TRAIN input normalization**. M0 uses behavior H=2 sequences only; M1 adds true-SOFA planner-plan sequences from the same episode split. The three fixed training seeds are 260312, 260313, 260314; no best seed is selected. M0/260312 reproduces the exact G9 E1/H=2 checkpoint with maximum weight difference **0** and identical validation correction.

## Fork validity, dataset, and freeze

The pre-collection two-step gate checked 16 selected states across four ordered archived episodes: 376 parent steps, 16 identical two-action repeat forks, and 16 archived behavior-action-pair forks. The parent and behavior-pair comparisons were exact for observation, applied action, reward, tip position, insertion, and distal force (maximum numerical difference **0**). Pre-action E1 history and frozen latents were checked at decision time. The full development collection then replayed seeds 90000–90199 **in one reused scene**, matching all **15,500** archived parent steps exactly; planner forks did not alter the parent. Confirmation likewise used one reused scene per ordered pass and matched all **2,291** first-pass parent steps exactly. These checks support two-step fork determinism and faithful planner capture; they do not prove the simulator is a physical force ground truth.

The frozen G3 progress/prior-force rule selected four states per development episode, covering early/middle/late episode progress and a high prior-force stratum without looking at candidate fork outcomes. The G3 episode-disjoint split was **140/30/30 episodes**, giving **560/120/120 planner states** for TRAIN/VALIDATION/DEVELOPMENT-TEST. All candidate plans from one state remain together. Development produced **800 states × 9 = 7,200 forks**, of which **7,193** have complete H=2 targets and **7** are censored. Complete rows by split: **5,038/1,080/1,075**. M0 behavior rows were 10,203 TRAIN and 2,136 VALIDATION; M1 added 5,038/1,080 planner rows. The development-test planner plans were held out from fitting and calibration.

`pre_confirmation_freeze.json` was written **before** any seed 103000–103029 ran (SHA256 `f90ead4c6ff1b5432bc57d121fdd44b19eb06e0792696643f59976eeb3a40f21`). It hashes the six checkpoints, old G9 checkpoint, E1/normalization and planner sources, sampling/evaluation scripts, development data and metrics, fixes H=2/quantiles/`F_lim`, and declares the ordered confirmation seeds. Confirmation data were neither used for training, checkpoint selection, recalibration, nor threshold choice. The first confirmation pass recorded 30 frozen-controller episodes (25 navigation successes, 2,291 steps) in one reused scene. Four states/episode were selected **before planner fork labels**. The second ordered pass captured and labeled 120 states × 9 actual planner plans: **1,080 complete R2 targets, zero censored**. The corresponding saved parent-step and state-selection audits passed. State selection uses completed behavior episodes to stratify prior force; the risk evaluation is prospective **with respect to captured candidate action sequences**, not an online state-selection policy.

The old G9 event-enriched 12-plan diagnostic rejected **9/12** truly safe plans, but that cohort is not exchangeable with these 120 broadly selected states. On the **same new plans**, the exact old G9 checkpoint rejects **164/923**, while M1 rejects **140/923, 160/923, or 124/923**. This is a modest, seed-dependent reduction, not evidence that the original event-specific 9/12 failure has been resolved. In fact the old model's new-cohort rejection rate is already far below 75%, so that prior extreme cannot be attributed **primarily** to planner-distribution mismatch from this evidence alone. It also reflects the event-enriched state mix and possibly conditional ambiguity.

## Decision

Planner-conditioned supervision lowers development-test unsafe acceptance and improves some untouched safe-plan decisions, so it is useful evidence against a wholly irreducible behavior-to-planner distribution problem. Yet the untouched benefit is inconsistent across seeds, unsafe acceptance reaches **11.5%** in the worst M1 replica, within-state ordering worsens in all three, and **13.2–15.1%** of genuinely feasible confirmation states are falsely predicted infeasible. The dominant **observed** blocker is conditional planner-plan risk discrimination/calibration at a state, including the safe-plan availability trade-off; these data do not isolate whether residual state-estimation error or physical ambiguity causes it. The nine-plan subset and many single-class states also limit claims about the full CEM population. No arbitrary numerical pass threshold was imposed.

**Final classification: B — planner-conditioned training helps but remains insufficient. G10 is not justified for automatic launch.** Stop after this offline prospective-plan study. Do not integrate a safety head, filter the live planner, change the solver/controller/force metric, or launch another experiment without a separate decision.

## Failure mechanism and decision

The predeclared mutually exclusive primary-cause priority is candidate-set infeasible, then predicted infeasible, unsafe pass-through, wrong repair, other. It yields G7 counts, respectively: **12/3/0/1/0** (260206), **12/4/0/0/0** (260207), **12/3/0/2/0** (260208), with all other states classified none. This taxonomy prioritizes structural infeasibility: the one raw unsafe pass-through for 260208 (state 110) is counted under candidate-set infeasible, not double-counted. Raw outcome counts remain in the table and per-state CSVs. Example repair failures are states 15 and 81; conservative-infeasible example IDs are in `infeasibility_analysis.csv` and full `failure_taxonomy.csv`.

**Dominant obstacle:** the frozen discrete candidate set contains no true-safe option in 12/16 unsafe-nominal states, and G7 additionally misses most of the four repairable opportunities through conservative infeasibility or wrong selection. G7 does not outperform G6B on this paired dataset: G6B rescues 1–2/4, has zero selected unsafe actions for all three replicas, but abstains in 16–19 states. The only recommended next methodological change is to **investigate and improve candidate-action coverage at true-infeasible selected states**, with a separately scoped study that first tests whether physically feasible safe actions exist beyond the frozen 21 candidates. Do not infer that such actions exist from these data. Do not proceed to G9 online control, retraining, recalibration, or controller modification under G8.
